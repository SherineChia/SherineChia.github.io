About Me

Hi! 👋 I’m Sherine, a student at Universitas International Batam (UIB), majoring in Information Systems. Honestly, I’m not an expert yet—but I’m still learning, growing, and figuring things out step by step.

After campus, my day continues at my workplace, Hanna Filia—an English course center filled with meaningful stories. It used to have around 800 students and was even featured in a newspaper! But now, it focuses more on social impact rather than business.

Here, students come from all backgrounds—locals, international students from China, and even refugees from Afghanistan. And guess what? Refugees can learn here for free. This happens because the owner truly cares. She couldn’t ignore their strong desire to learn English despite having limited opportunities. Even after external funding stopped, Hanna Filia continues to support them.

So, what do I do there? A bit of everything 😄—admin, cleaning, and especially as a substitute teacher. The role I enjoy the most is teaching—especially refugee kids aged 7–10 in the evening. It’s one of the most meaningful parts of my day. That’s a little story about me ✨ Curious to know more? Let’s explore my biodata website! 😊
